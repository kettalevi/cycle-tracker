Reminders

Notifications appear when you open the app, because it has no server to send them while closed. For reminders that arrive even when the app is closed, add the predicted dates to your phone's calendar app. Download the file and open it, and your calendar will remind you.

Defaults are used until you have logged a couple of cycles; after that your own averages take over (except luteal phase).

Please note

Cycle Tracker gives estimates based on your logged dates and general averages. It is not a medical device, is not reliable contraception, and is no substitute for professional advice. See a doctor if your cycles are very irregular, very painful, or you are concerned about your health.
